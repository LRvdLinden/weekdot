Privacy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Weekdot is built to keep your data yours.

Calendar data: Weekdot reads and writes events only in the calendar accounts on your device (the Calendar app). Your events are never sent to us.

iCloud: Weekdot's own settings, templates, calendar sets, rules, colours and emoji are stored in your private iCloud so they sync between your devices. We cannot access them.

Weather (optional): when you turn on weather, Weekdot asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for a forecast for your approximate location. Weekdot does not store it.

Homey (optional): when you link Weekdot to Homey, you sign in with your Homey account and Weekdot sends your events of the coming 1, 2 or 3 months (you choose), from the calendars you choose, to the Weekdot app on your own Homey, through Homey's own connection. Weekdot keeps the sign-in only on your device and does not keep a copy of your events. You can hide titles and places, and sign out at any time.

Video calls (optional): when you add a Google Meet or Microsoft Teams meeting to an event, you sign in with that Google or Microsoft account and Weekdot asks Google or Microsoft to create a meeting link (for Teams with the event title and time). Weekdot keeps the sign-in only on your device, can only see meetings it created itself and does not read your mail, contacts or other calendars. Join buttons for links already in your events work on your device without any account. You can sign out at any time in Settings › Video Calls.

Google user data: Weekdot only requests permission to create Google Meet meeting spaces (meetings.space.created) and your e-mail address to show which account is signed in. Weekdot uses this only to create a meeting link at your request, does not store it anywhere other than in the event you save, does not share it with anyone, does not use it for advertising and does not use it to train AI models. Weekdot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google API Services User Data Policy

No accounts, no tracking, no ads and no analytics.

Cookies

This website only uses storage that is needed: it remembers your cookie choice for 12 months, in a cookie called weekdot_consent and in your browser's local storage. If you open a link with a language in it, that language is remembered until you close the browser.

Only when you choose Accept do we load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser then connects to Google, which can see your IP address. With Necessary only, the site uses your device's own system font. No ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
