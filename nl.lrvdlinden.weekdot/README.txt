Let your calendar run your home. Your events arrive on Homey, so Flows can start when an appointment begins or ends, minutes or days before it, or with today's agenda. Filter on a calendar or a word in the title, use the details as tags, and put your day, week or month on your dashboard with seven widgets.

Works with the free Weekdot app for iPhone, iPad or Mac, where you choose which calendars are shared; your events stay on your own Homey. For questions and help, visit the Homey Community topic of this app.
